# Appendix B. Open Questions for the School

These are the questions I could not answer from what the school gave me. Each one has a default beside it, and the default is what the build will do if the answer does not arrive, so none of these blocks work from starting. What they do affect is how much rework happens after the school sees the first result sheet, and question 1 is the one that matters most. The school should mark each row either agreed or changed and return the table.

| No. | Question | What I will build if I get no answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The result sheet.** I still do not have GRAS's own sheet. What I now have is a screenshot of another school's online result, used as a floor plan and written up in Appendix C.1 and C.9. Is that grouping close enough to what GRAS wants, and which of the fields Appendix C makes optional, such as per-subject position or the subject teacher's remark, should actually appear? A photograph or a scan of one completed GRAS sheet, if one exists on paper already, or a plain description of what should differ from the reference, answers this fully. | The header grouping, subject table shape, side panels and inline annual summary described in Appendix C.1 through C.9, built in GRAS's own branding. Expect to revise the layout once after the school sees the first draft. Everything referenced is already stored, so a wording or ordering change is a template edit rather than new development. |
| 2 | Does the school want the pupil's photograph printed on the paper result sheet, and separately, on the sheet a parent downloads from the portal? ## Appendix B, part 2: questions arising from the school's own forms

Question 1 in the table above is now largely answered: the school has supplied its own nursery and primary sheets, its weekly sheet and its admission form. What it has not supplied is confirmation of several values that had to be read off a scan, plus decisions the forms raise but do not settle.

The nine numbered items in `25-open-conflicts-to-resolve.md` are the substantive ones and are not repeated here. The questions below are additional and smaller.

| No. | Question | What I will build if I get no answer |
|---|---|---|
| 19 | ~~Confirm the transcribed indicator and trait lists.~~ **Answered.** The school confirms both lists as transcribed, including `Home work on High Quality`. The school may also add its own indicators and whole domains through 6.2.13. | Closed. |
| 20 | The nursery sheet carries no attendance and the primary sheet does. Is that deliberate, or should nursery print it too? | Nursery captures attendance and does not print it. Adding it to the nursery sheet later is a template change. |
