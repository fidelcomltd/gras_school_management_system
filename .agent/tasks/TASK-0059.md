## Goal

The system can answer "which arm is this pupil in, and since when" — and "who sat in Primary 2A on
12/01/2027". An `enrolment` row joins one pupil to one arm with an effective-from date and an
optional effective-to date. This is the spine the rest of the product hangs off: admission
approval, transfers, promotion, capacity, score sheets, results and the parent portal all resolve
a pupil's class through it.

## Contract delta

None. See header.

## The one thing that must not be got wrong

`02-data-model.md` section 5.2, near-verbatim: *a `pupil` row does not carry a level or an arm
column. Asking which arm a pupil is in is a query against `enrolment` for the open row. This is the
single most important normalisation in the schema and shortcutting it with a `pupil.arm_id` column
will break transfers, promotion and historical results at the same time.*

Do not add `arm_id` or `class_level_id` to `pupil`, not even as a denormalised cache.
