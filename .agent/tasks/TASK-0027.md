## §5 sign-off — GRANTED 2026-09-06

Two of the seven endpoints are session/credential operations: `POST /admins/{id}/password-reset`
(mints a new temporary password, revokes every session) and `DELETE /admins/{id}/sessions`.
Suspension and deactivation also revoke sessions. CLAUDE.md §5 required a human ruling on who may
operate that on whose behalf; the mechanism is TASK-0003's and unchanged.

**The human approved the delta as drafted.** The privilege grant is the whole gate: an account
holding `admin.password.reset` / `admin.session.revoke` may exercise it against another account.
**No step-up re-authentication** of the acting admin, and **no extra `is_super_admin` requirement**
on those two endpoints — do not add either. The only guards are the ones already in the acceptance
criteria below. Full ruling: `decisions/2026-Q3-contract-deltas.md`, entry `TASK-0019/0027`, Part 2.

## Goal

A Super Admin creates, edits, suspends, reactivates, deactivates, force-resets and signs out
administrator accounts, with the invariants spec 4.1 and 6.1.7 rule 4 require.
