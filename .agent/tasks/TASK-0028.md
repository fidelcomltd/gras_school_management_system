# TASK-0028 — Roles, assignments and the privilege register surface

Owner: backend-dev
Depends on: TASK-0027
Contract impact: additive — role and assignment endpoints, plus additive fields on existing shapes
Status: queued — **card is a stub; write it out before dispatch**

## Goal

Real role assignment: role CRUD, granting and revoking assignments over sessions and scopes, the
read-only privilege register, and the three privilege-escalation rules that only exist once
assignments do.

## Why it is a separate card

TASK-0003 provisionally ruled Super Admin to be a flag that bypasses privilege checks, and named
"real role assignment" as TASK-0019's. On 2026-09-06 the orchestrator moved it here: spec 6.1.7's
rules 1-3 are all about `role_assignment` mutation and role privilege lists, so they cannot be
tested at all until this surface exists, and folding them into the accounts card puts it far over
the ~400-line dispatch limit (§1). **The flag-bypass ruling stays provisional until this closes.**

## Scope sketch (expand before dispatch)

- Spec 6.1.14: `GET /privileges` · `GET|POST /roles` · `GET|PATCH|DELETE /roles/{id}` (PATCH on a
  system role returns 409) · `GET|POST /admins/{id}/assignments` · `DELETE /assignments/{id}` ·
  `POST /assignments/copy-to-session` with a dry-run flag.
- 6.1.7 rules 1, 2 and 3, each with a test per escalation path, **each writing an audit event on
  rejection** (6.1.7 preamble).
- The additive fields TASK-0027 deliberately omitted: `rolesHeld`/`scopeSummary` on the account
  list item; assignments, resolved effective privileges and last-ten-audit-events on the detail
  view; the role / scope-arm / session list filters (6.1.8).
- **`/auth/me` gains assignments and the resolved effective privilege set with scopes** — spec
  6.1.14's `GET /me`. Additive to an endpoint TASK-0003 shipped; flag it in the delta as such.
- Deactivation's assignment-revocation half, left as a seam by TASK-0027 (6.1.10) — and
  reactivation must NOT restore them.
- `IEffectivePrivilegeProvider` graduates from the super-admin-flag-only implementation TASK-0003
  shipped to one that actually resolves assignments.
