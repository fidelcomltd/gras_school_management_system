# TASK-0028 — Roles and the privilege register

Owner: backend-dev
Depends on: TASK-0027 (closed)
Contract impact: additive — three new paths (`/privileges`, `/roles`, `/roles/{id}`), six operations
Status: **done** (closed 2026-09-07 by orchestrator; gates re-run by the orchestrator, not accepted on report)

## Goal

An administrator can read the privilege register grouped by module, and create, list, read, edit
and remove roles, with spec 6.1.7 rule 2 enforced on every privilege a role gains. Six seeded roles
ship with the system. Nothing in this card grants anything to anyone — that is TASK-0030.

## Why assignments are NOT in this card

The stub sketched roles **and** `role_assignment` together. `role_assignment` (6.1.5) requires
`session_id` referencing an academic session and `arm_ids` referencing arms; **neither entity
exists** — `Domain/` holds Auth, Common, Idempotency, Reference, Security, Settings and nothing
else. Building assignments now means unvalidated opaque GUIDs, no closed-session rejection
(6.1.13), no "every arm must belong to session_id" (6.1.5) — i.e. shipping the escalation surface
with its validation missing. Split on 2026-09-06: **TASK-0030** carries assignments, scopes,
6.1.7 rules 1 and 3, the additive account-list/detail/`/auth/me` fields, deactivation's revocation
half and `IEffectivePrivilegeProvider`'s graduation, and is blocked on the sessions and arms cards.
`role` itself has no session or arm reference, so it is cleanly buildable today.
**The super-admin flag-bypass ruling stays provisional until TASK-0030 closes, not this card.**
