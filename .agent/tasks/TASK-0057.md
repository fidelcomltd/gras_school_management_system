## Notes for the implementing agent

**The numbering is immutable, and this is the one way this card can do real damage.** Sixty-five
files cite these section numbers, including `.agent/AUDIT.md`, the decision and drift archives, the
contract-delta archive, and `.agent/contract-history.md`. Citations in the wild span §2.2, §2.3,
§2.4, §2.9, §2.14, §2.16, §2.19, §2.20, §2.23, §2.25, §2.27, §3.1, §3.8 and §3.9. **A renumber
silently redirects every one of them to the wrong subject** — and because they are prose citations,
nothing will fail to compile and no gate will go red. Numbers are permanent identifiers here, not
ordering. If two sections ought to merge, they do not merge.

**Verify the numbering mechanically, before and after:**

```

diff <(sed 's/:.*### /|/' /tmp/before.txt) <(sed 's/:.*### /|/' /tmp/after.txt)
```

Paste that diff in your report. Section numbers and subjects must correspond one-to-one; only line
numbers may move.
