## Goal

`GET /pupils` returns pupils in 6.5.15's class-progression order — level order, then arm, then
surname, then id — instead of today's surname-then-id. This is the half of 6.5.15 that has been
live drift since 2026-09-09, deferred out of TASK-0059 as its pre-authorised cut line.

## Contract delta

None. See header.

## The open question — a human must answer before this is dispatched

**Where does a pupil with no open enrolment sort?**

`active` pupils normally have one, but the states that legitimately produce none include
`transferred`, `withdrawn` and `graduated` (6.5.14 closes the enrolment on each), and `pending`
records never have one. 6.5.15 describes the ordering for pupils who ARE in a class and says
nothing about pupils who are not. The plausible answers — first, last, excluded entirely, or
ordered by their most recent CLOSED enrolment — are all defensible and produce visibly different
screens.

This is product behaviour, and `CLAUDE.md` section 1 forbids inventing it. TASK-0059's implementing
agent hit exactly this and stopped rather than guessing, which is the behaviour the rules ask for.

## Notes for the implementing agent

where off-by-one page seams come from, hence the across-page-boundary criterion above.

not from alphabetical level names — "Primary 10" must not sort between "Primary 1" and "Primary 2".
