## Goal

A teacher with `result.compute` runs computation on one arm's result set, and the three computed
tables are rewritten from its marks: `subject_result_line`, `subject_arm_statistic` and
`pupil_term_result`. Proven by §8.4 loaded as an integration fixture. Nothing reads the rows over
HTTP yet; the broadsheet and pupil-result cards do.

## Human rulings (2026-09-17), binding

1. **Step 10 crosses arms.** Rank the whole level from every arm's live, complete, non-voided marks,
   computed in memory; write `level_position`/`level_pupil_count` **only for this set's pupils**. Never
   read siblings' stored rows, never write or flag sibling sets. Stored sibling level positions may go
   stale; the publication card re-checks them (drift entry). Low stakes today: §6.7.12's amendment
   prints no position on either sheet.
2. **Draft computation over incomplete rows.** Literal §8.3: an incomplete or missing row writes no subject
   line and adds 0 to `total_obtained`; `subjects_taken` stays the in-effect count; the pupil is excluded
   from that subject's counted and ranked populations; "scored subjects" for `minSubjectsForPosition` =
   complete rows. The submission gate makes this unreachable for a submitted set.
