## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] §8.2 steps 1-11 implemented in order. **Steps 5 and 6 stay separate passes** — collapsing
      them is the named mistake that puts an absentee into a class average.
- [ ] §8.3 formulas exact, including rounding **half up** at the stated precision: class average to
      1dp, average to 2dp, cumulative to 2dp.
- [ ] Competition ranking: equal values share a rank and the next rank skips by the number tied.
- [ ] A subject_total matching no grading band **stops the whole computation** (§8.2 step 4).
- [ ] Step 11 is one transaction: delete existing computed rows, write new, clear needs_recompute.
- [ ] §8.4 loaded as an integration fixture with every assertion §8.5 lists.
- [ ] **The two coverage gaps §8.5 names are closed by unit tests**, not by a second fixture: no
      total in §8.4 lands in band **D (40-49)** or band **F (0-19)**, so band resolution at the
      bottom of the scale is otherwise unexercised. F matters most — it is the band this spec added
      rather than the school, and a contiguity regression would surface there first.
- [ ] No hard-coded component count and no literal 40 anywhere, per TASK-0069's durability rule.
- [ ] Contract delta approved before implementation; drift gate green.
- [ ] Gates green via `-IntegrationFilter` scoped to your classes. Never the full gate.
