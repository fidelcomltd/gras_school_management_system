## Why this card matters more than its size suggests

`SuperAdminFlagEffectivePrivilegeProvider` currently short-circuits privilege resolution on a
boolean flag. It has been live since TASK-0002 and every authorization test in the codebase runs
against it. **This card is what makes TASK-0003's flag-bypass ruling final.** Until it lands, the
project's authorization story is a placeholder.

## Contract delta

All additive. Privilege constants already exist (`Role.Assign`, `Role.ScopeAssign`).

| Endpoint | Privilege | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/v1/admins/{id}/assignments` | `admin.view` | The account's assignments with role, scope type, scope ids, session. |
| `POST /api/v1/admins/{id}/assignments` | `role.assign`, or `role.scope.assign` for an arm-scoped grant | `Idempotency-Key` REQUIRED. Subject to rules 1 and 3. |
| `DELETE /api/v1/assignments/{id}` | `role.assign` | Subject to rule 1. |

`role_assignment` per spec 6.1.5: `admin_account_id`, `role_id`, `scope_type`, `arm_ids`,
`session_id`. Every arm in `arm_ids` must belong to `session_id` — **that validation is now
writable for the first time, and it is the reason this card waited.**

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `role_assignment` persisted with the 6.1.5 shape. **Every arm in `arm_ids` belongs to
      `session_id`**, enforced and tested with a cross-session arm that must be rejected.
- [x] **Rule 1** — no account may create, edit or revoke an assignment where `admin_account_id`
      equals its own id. Verbatim message: `You cannot change your own roles. Ask another Super
      Admin.` Tested on all three verbs, not just create.
- [x] **Rule 3** — no account may grant a scope wider than its own. An arm-scoped holder of
      `role.scope.assign` may assign only over arms inside its own scope; school-wide assignment
      requires `role.assign`, which is never granted arm-scoped. Tested both directions: a
      permitted narrower grant succeeds, a wider one is rejected.
- [x] **Every rejection under rules 1 and 3 writes an audit event with `outcome = rejected`**
      (spec 6.1.7 preamble, 6.1.12). Assert the row exists after a rejected attempt — an attempt
      must be visible even though it failed. This is the half most easily forgotten.
- [x] **`IEffectivePrivilegeProvider` resolves real assignments**, and
      `SuperAdminFlagEffectivePrivilegeProvider` is **deleted, not left registered behind a flag**.
      A test proves a non-super-admin's effective privileges come from their assignments, and that
      a super admin still resolves everything (rule 4 — `is_super_admin` remains settable only by
      an account that has it, and is in no role's privilege list).
- [x] Deactivating an account revokes its assignments; **reactivation does NOT restore them**
      (6.1.10) — the seam TASK-0027 left. Both directions tested.
- [x] Existing authorization tests still pass against the real provider. If any test only passed
      because of the flag bypass, **report it rather than adapting the test to keep it green.**
- [x] `openapi.json` regenerated via `scripts/generate-openapi.ps1 -Promote`; `CONTRACT.lock`
      updated; all ten §9 gates green, `SUMMARY` pasted.
