## Why it is blocked, not merely queued

`role_assignment` (6.1.5) requires `session_id` referencing an academic session and `arm_ids`
referencing arms. Neither entity exists. Without them these validations cannot be written at all,
only faked: "every arm must belong to `session_id`" (6.1.5), "the session 2025/2026 is closed"
(6.1.13), the arm-deletion cascade that revokes an emptied assignment (6.1.13), and 6.1.7 rule 3's
"no scope wider than your own". Shipping the escalation surface with its validation stubbed is
worse than shipping it later. Split out of TASK-0028 on 2026-09-06.
